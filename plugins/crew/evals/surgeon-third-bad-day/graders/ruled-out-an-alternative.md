The symptom invites several causes: a duplicated observation, a mood saved wrongly, an observation logged against the wrong pupil, a threshold or scoring mistake. The real cause is the date window. The Surgeon's report lists what it ruled out and how.

PASS if the response names at least one of those alternatives (or any other specific candidate cause) and says it was ruled out, with the evidence: for example "no duplicate rows", "all three moods are dysregulated in the database", "the threshold is three as specified", or a query or test that checked it.

FAIL if the response names only the cause it settled on, with no alternative considered, or if it lists alternatives without saying they were checked.
