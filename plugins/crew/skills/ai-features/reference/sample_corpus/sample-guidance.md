## Requesting an assessment

When a parent or a school asks the local authority to assess a pupil's
needs, the local authority must decide whether to carry out the assessment
and tell the parent of its decision within six weeks of the request.

## Recording a concern

A concern about a pupil is recorded on the day it is observed, in the words
of the person who observed it, and shared with the designated lead the same
day. The record is never edited afterwards; a correction is a new entry.

## Reviewing support

Support put in place for a pupil is reviewed at least once a term with the
pupil's parents, and the review notes what changed and what was agreed.
